| Source | Identity and use |
| --- | --- |
| Tian–Qi, *Expected values for the rational complexity of finite binary sequences* | DCC 55 (2010), 65–79, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-009-9331-x). Same ordinary mean; weaker upper, short primitive counts and high-valuation family. |
| Chen–Winterhof, *Probabilistic results on the 2-adic complexity* | [arXiv:2501.16785v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16785v1), 28 January 2025. Theorems 2–3 give a lower N/2−1 and an upper with logarithmic error. Detailed locators use this version. |
| Chen–Winterhof, journal version | DCC 93 (2025), 2191–2203, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-025-01592-1). Bibliographic identity and abstract inspected; full body unread. No theorem-by-theorem equivalence with v1 is asserted. |
| Lin–Xiao–Chen, *A brief view on the linear and 2-adic complexities of pseudorandom binary sequences* | AIMS Mathematics 11(7) (2026), 20267–20290, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3934/math.2026823). Question 3.1, p.20282, asks the exact mean upper and a separate variance question. |
| Ren–Winterhof, *Symmetric measures of pseudorandomness for binary sequences* | [arXiv:2603.23166v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23166v1), 24 March 2026. Symmetric reversal complexity is a different random variable. |
| Goresky–Klapper, *Algebraic Shift Register Sequences* | Cambridge (2012), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139057448). CW credits published Lemma 18.5.1 for growth. | Cassels, *An Introduction to the Geometry of Numbers* | Springer 1997 reprint, Chapter III §1, [chapter DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-62035-5_4). Minkowski theorem used with strict volume, followed by discreteness. |

The theorem answers the mean part of the survey question. No exact antecedent
was identified in the primary portions inspected; this is a bounded source
comparison rather than a certification of universal priority.
The unread journal final body remains a limit to that comparison.
The proofs in the manuscript supply the specific finite estimates autonomously.
