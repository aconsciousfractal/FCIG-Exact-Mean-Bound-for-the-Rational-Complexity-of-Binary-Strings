# Known limits

- The theorem concerns the uniform ordinary unrounded logarithmic mean over all
  finite binary strings. It does not supply an upper bound for each string.
- The variance part of Question 3.1 remains open here.
- No statement about every prescribed infinite 2-adic number or Collatz orbit
  follows from this mean bound.
- The proof is hybrid. Exact finite certificates are essential for parent
  lengths 1–25; the analytic argument covers parent lengths at least 26.
- A successful finite checker run does not validate all analytic proof steps.
- The full Chen–Winterhof journal final body was not read. Universal originality,
  firstness and priority are not certified by this source comparison.
- AI-assisted reviews had shared exposure; human peer review and formal proof
  verification are separate matters.
- Hosted CI execution is not inferred from the presence of a workflow.
